Hi, this is My FlashEats Track A project. I started with the business question: late deliveries are frustrating customers. But before predicting them, we need to know where time is being spent and whether the record supports a decision.

The classroom pack gives us three double modes.

I kept the provided source files in the repo so this can be rerun. The data is controlled teaching datasets so I am careful not to describe the figures as live FlashEats performance.
